How to play

Turns

Blue opens by placing 1 tile. After that the players alternate placing 2 tiles per turn: Red 2, Blue 2, Red 2, and so on. A tile can go on any empty hex — the board is infinite.

Health

A new tile starts with 1 health. At the end of every turn, every tile on the board gains:

  • +1 per neighbor of its own color (up to +6), and
  • + the size of its blob — the connected group of same-colored tiles it belongs to, counting itself.

Attacks

Every pair of your tiles on the same line (any of the hex grid's 3 axes) deals 2 damage to each enemy tile between them, at the end of every turn. Every pair counts, and tiles in between don't break a pair. Example: with Blue tiles at positions 1, 2, 4 and 5 and a Red tile at 3, there are 4 pairs around the Red tile (1–4, 1–5, 2–4, 2–5), so it takes 8 damage. Each of the 3 axes adds its own damage.

End of turn

Growth and damage are both worked out from the board as it stands, then applied together. Any tile at 0 health or below is destroyed. Your score is the total health of all your tiles.

Badges

Each tile previews what the end of this turn will do to it: + from neighbors (top left), + from blob size (top right) and − damage (bottom, in the attacker's color). Zero-value badges are hidden.

Settings

Damage and empty-hex rules were chosen when this online game was created.

Controls

  • Click or tap an empty hex to place a tile.
  • Drag to pan. Scroll, pinch, or + / − to zoom. H fits all tiles in view.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes your last placement. Online, you can take back a tile until your turn is finished.
  • Online games are saved on the server: reopen the link any time to continue. Rematch clears the board and swaps colors.
